# NammaVaani Farm Companion — 90-second demo script

**0–10 seconds — Problem and introduction**

“Farmers need crop information, weather, market prices and equipment support, but many digital services require typing or navigating separate applications. NammaVaani brings these tasks into one voice-first companion.”

**10–25 seconds — Voice and language**

“The farmer can choose from ten Gnani-supported Indian speech locales. Prisma v2.5 uses the selected language for each recording. If the account is configured for Prisma v3.0, optional Auto Detect can identify supported Indic speech and use the code-mixed output format. Kannada, Telugu and English have built-in full workflows. Seven additional languages can use an optional translation bridge; without it they are clearly marked speech-beta. We do not claim untested dialects or code-mixing combinations are fully supported.”

**25–40 seconds — My Farm and Crop Planner**

“The farmer saves their village, land size, soil and water source once. The Crop Planner shows crop groups documented for UAS Bengaluru Zone V. It does not pretend to rank the best crop without soil tests, sowing-window and current local guidance.”

**40–55 seconds — Weather**

“The farmer can retrieve a five-day forecast for their selected town. We show the data source, retrieval time and a warning that a forecast is guidance, not a guarantee for an individual field.”

**55–70 seconds — Mandi prices**

“The operator uploads the official Agmarknet CSV—no mandi API key is needed. The app filters actual market observations and shows the market, arrival date and freshness label. If records are unavailable, we say so rather than inventing a price.”

**70–82 seconds — Farm repairs and operator**

“The original repair workflow remains available. A farmer can describe an equipment problem, review the extracted details and confirm a ticket. The operator can update its status.”

**82–90 seconds — Innovation**

“Our contribution is the application layer around Gnani: a saved farm profile, conversational clarification, source-backed services, data freshness checks and a simple spoken experience.”


**Language demo note:** Show a Kannada conversation as the primary live demo. A Telugu turn may be shown as the second full-workflow language. Only demonstrate the additional seven languages as complete task flows if the optional translation endpoint is configured and a fluent speaker has reviewed the output. Otherwise show the beta notice honestly.
